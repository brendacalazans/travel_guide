🇵🇪 Peru • Sacred Andes South America

Cusco & Sacred Valley

The navel of the Inca universe and living Quechua cosmology

Why It Is Essential

Ancient capital of the Inca Empire (Tawantinsuyu), Cusco marries megalithic stone foundations with Spanish colonial woodwork, keeping ancient Quechua agricultural wisdom vibrant in Pisac and Ollantaytambo.

🍲 Ancestral Flavors
  • • Highland Trout Ceviche: Glacial river fish cured in citrus leche de tigre with giant choclo corn.
  • • Lomo Saltado: Stir-fried beef tenderloin with ají amarillo and Cantonese wok roots.
  • • Chicha Morada: Refreshing purple corn brew infused with cloves, cinnamon, and spiced apple.
🕯️ Living Lore & Curiosity

During Pachamama Raymi (Mother Earth month in August), locals place coca leaves and grains into anti-seismic stone fissures, celebrating cosmic equilibrium and ecological renewal.

🥾 Essential Experiences

Boarding the panoramic train to Machu Picchu; walking the stepped salineras of Maras; discovering Qorikancha Sun Temple.

7-Day Estimated Total $1,480 USD

🇨🇴 Colombia • Caribbean South America

Cartagena & Rosario Islands

Colonial ramparts, champeta beats, and magical realism

Why It Is Essential

Cartagena guards the maritime heart of the Colombian Caribbean. Beyond bougainvillea balconies and Getsemaní murals, the dignity of palenqueras and Gabo’s literary legacy come alive.

🍲 Ancestral Flavors
  • • Arepa de Huevo: Twice-fried golden corn pocket filled with an egg and seasoned ground meat.
  • • Pargo Rojo con Arroz con Coco: Crispy whole red snapper served with coconut rice and fried patacones.
  • • Posta Negra Cartagenera: Slow-simmered beef in a caramelized panela sugarcane and allspice reduction.
🕯️ Living Lore & Curiosity

Just outside the city lies San Basilio de Palenque, the first free town in the Americas founded by escaped enslaved people in 1603, boasting its own Creole tongue and polyrhythmic drum culture.

🥾 Essential Experiences

Sunset viewing over the historic ramparts at Café del Mar; street art walking tour in Getsemaní; speedboat journey to the turquoise waters of Barú and Rosario Islands.

7-Day Estimated Total $1,320 USD
Flight (Est.) $420 USD
Hotel / night $70 - $120
Food / day $25 - $40
🇨🇱 Chile • Altiplano South America

Atacama Desert

The clearest cosmos on Earth and Licanantay lore

Why It Is Essential

The driest non-polar desert on Earth surprises with turquoise salt lakes dotted with flamingos, bubbling geyser fields at 4,300m, and adobe hamlets preserved under zero light pollution.

🍲 Ancestral Flavors
  • • Pastel de Choclo: Sweet baked corn pie layered over seasoned beef, chicken, and black olives.
  • • Rica-Rica Herbal Gelato: Dessert infused with the fragrant wild altiplano mint-sage shrub.
  • • Cazuela de Llama / Trout: Comforting earthenware hotpots made with highland vegetables.
🕯️ Living Lore & Curiosity

The Licanantay people read the "Dark Constellations"—the cosmic silhouettes of interstellar dust inside the Milky Way—looking for the Celestial Llama to set the planting cycle.

🥾 Essential Experiences

Nighttime deep-sky telescope tour; relaxing in Puritama thermal pools; watching the dawn steam plumes at El Tatio Geysers.

7-Day Estimated Total $1,790 USD
Flight (Est.) $680 USD
Hotel / night $90 - $160
Food / day $35 - $60
🇧🇴 Bolivia • Altiplano South America

Salar de Uyuni & Potosí

The world’s largest water mirror and silver lore

Why It Is Essential

Over 10,000 km² of brilliant white salt that becomes an infinite mirror during the rainy season. Coupled with Potosí’s historic Cerro Rico, it reveals the historical pulse and Aymara soul of Bolivia.

🍲 Ancestral Flavors
  • • Bolivian Salteñas: Golden braided pastries filled with seasoned piping-hot broth and beef.
  • • Royal Quinoa Chowder: Sacred altiplano grain simmered with Andean vegetables and muña herbs.
  • • Pique Macho: Mountain-sized platter of sliced beef tenderloin, sausage, fries, and spicy locoto.
🕯️ Living Lore & Curiosity

On Incahuasi Island, 700-year-old giant cacti grow over prehistoric fossilized coral. In Potosí, miners still offer coca leaves and spirits to El Tío, guardian deity of the deep earth.

🥾 Essential Experiences

3-day 4x4 expedition to the Red and Green lagoons; sleeping in a hotel built entirely with salt blocks; optical perspective photography on the salt desert.

7-Day Estimated Total $1,290 USD
Flight (Est.) $650 USD
Hotel / night $45 - $90
Food / day $15 - $30
🇦🇷 Argentina • Cuyo South America

Mendoza & The Andes

Malbec in the shadow of the mighty Aconcagua

Why It Is Essential

An oasis irrigated by ancient channels engineered by the indigenous Huarpe people, crowned by the glaciated peaks of the Andes and home to the world's most acclaimed high-altitude Malbecs.

🍲 Ancestral Flavors
  • • Asado Criollo: Prime beef grilled patiently over fragrant vine wood embers with fresh chimichurri.
  • • Empanadas Mendocinas: Flaky pastry stuffed with knife-cut beef, sweet onions, and green olives.
  • • Alfajor de Maizena: Delicate cornstarch cookies filled with dulce de leche and rolled in shredded coconut.
🕯️ Living Lore & Curiosity

The Fiesta Nacional de la Vendimia (each March) is one of the southern hemisphere's oldest harvest festivals, honoring how glacial meltwaters are transformed through community care into celebrated wine.

🥾 Essential Experiences

Biking between vineyard estates in Uco Valley; hiking to the South Face viewpoint in Aconcagua Provincial Park; hot springs soak in Cacheuta.

7-Day Estimated Total $1,410 USD
Flight (Est.) $620 USD
Hotel / night $65 - $130
Food / day $25 - $45
🇧🇷 Brazil • Minas Gerais South America

Ouro Preto & Tiradentes

Soapstone baroque, Afro-Brazilian freedom, and cozy woodstoves

Why It Is Essential

A UNESCO World Heritage treasure where steep cobblestone streets preserve the sculpture genius of Aleijadinho and the resilience of enslaved African miners who funded their own brotherhood churches.

🍲 Ancestral Flavors
  • • Feijão Tropeiro: Brown beans tossed with toasted manioc flour, pork crackling, collard greens, and eggs.
  • • Frango com Quiabo: Chicken slow-cooked in a traditional soapstone pan served with creamy polenta (angu).
  • • Artisanal Minas Cheese: Raw milk cheese aged on native wood planks with fresh pour-over coffee.
🕯️ Living Lore & Curiosity

The legend of Chico Rei tells of an enslaved African monarch who bought freedom for himself, his son, and dozens of workers by hiding gold flakes in his hair, subsequently commissioning the grand Church of Santa Efigênia.

🥾 Essential Experiences

Touring São Francisco de Assis church with ceilings by Mestre Ataíde; historic steam train to Mariana; visiting soapstone artisans.

7-Day Estimated Total $1,450 USD
Flight (Est.) $750 USD
Hotel / night $55 - $110
Food / day $20 - $35
🇪🇨 Ecuador • Pacific South America

Galápagos & Quito

The living evolutionary cradle and equatorial history

Why It Is Essential

Quito preserves the best-maintained colonial historic center in the Americas, while the volcanic Galápagos archipelago offers face-to-face encounters with wildlife found nowhere else on the globe.

🍲 Ancestral Flavors
  • • Encebollado: Sustaining tuna soup made with cassava and tangy lime-marinated red onions.
  • • Locro de Papa: Velvety traditional Andean potato soup served with fresh cheese and avocado.
  • • Ceviche de Camarón: Sweet-and-tangy shrimp ceviche with citrus tomato broth and green plantain chifles.
🕯️ Living Lore & Curiosity

The giant tortoises that inspired Charles Darwin can live past 150 years. Shell shapes evolved distinctly on each island to match the precise height of local flora.

🥾 Essential Experiences

Snorkeling alongside sea lions in Los Túneles; standing with feet in both hemispheres at Mitad del Mundo; cable car ascent on Pichincha Volcano.

7-Day Estimated Total $2,250 USD
Flight + Island $750 USD
Hotel / night $110 - $210
Food / day $35 - $60
🇧🇷 Brazil • Maranhão South America

Lençóis & São Luís

Rolling quartz dunes, rainwater oases, and Afro-folkloric drums

Why It Is Essential

A singular geological phenomenon: 155,000 hectares of sweeping white dunes embracing thousands of emerald seasonal rainwater lagoons, alongside São Luís, a cultural capital adorned with Azulejo ceramic tiles.

🍲 Ancestral Flavors
  • • Arroz de Cuxá: Indigenous rice cooked with tart vinagreira roselle greens, dried shrimp, and sesame.
  • • Maranhense Shrimp Pie: Savory baked pie filled with coastal herbs, shrimp, and coconut milk.
  • • Bacuri & Cupuaçu Sorbet: Fragrant native wild fruits from the Amazon-Cerrado transition.
🕯️ Living Lore & Curiosity

The Bumba Meu Boi and the fire-warmed rhythm of Tambor de Crioula are UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritages, celebrating community rebirth through song and swirling skirts.

🥾 Essential Experiences

Swimming in Lagoa Bonita and Lagoa Azul; floating down the crystalline Formiga River in Cardosa; wandering the colonial center of São Luís.

7-Day Estimated Total $1,490 USD
Flight (Est.) $760 USD
Hotel / night $60 - $120
Food / day $20 - $35
🇬🇹 Guatemala • Highlands Central America

Antigua & Lake Atitlán

Sacred crater volcanoes and ancestral backstrap weaving

Why It Is Essential

Framed by three volcanoes, Atitlán is ringed by twelve villages keeping Kaqchikel and Tz’utujil tongues alive with handmade textiles. Nearby Antigua pairs colonial ruins with artisanal single-origin chocolate.

🍲 Ancestral Flavors
  • • Pepian de Pollo: Guatemala’s national Maya stew: chicken in a roasted squash seed, sesame, and mild chili sauce.
  • • Kak’ik: Ceremonial red turkey soup flavored with achiote, wild mint, and coriander.
  • • Rellenitos de Plátano: Sweet plantain fritters stuffed with chocolate-infused black bean paste.
🕯️ Living Lore & Curiosity

In Santiago Atitlán, locals venerate Maximón (San Simón), a syncretic folk saint and deity who receives cigars, colorful candles, and spirits in exchange for family protection.

🥾 Essential Experiences

Lancha boat tour between lakeside villages; botanical natural dye weaving workshop; roasting marshmallows on Pacaya Volcano’s lava fields.

7-Day Estimated Total $1,260 USD
Flight (Est.) $380 USD
Hotel / night $55 - $100
Food / day $20 - $35
🇨🇷 Costa Rica • Cloud Forest Central America

Monteverde & Nicoya

Pura Vida philosophy and cloud canopy biodiversity

Why It Is Essential

A world pioneer in regenerative ecotourism and renewable energy, Costa Rica harbors over 5% of the planet's biodiversity. Monteverde envelops you in hanging canopy bridges nestled within clouds.

🍲 Ancestral Flavors
  • • Gallo Pinto: Black beans and rice tossed with Salsa Lizano, fresh cilantro, and sweet peppers.
  • • Casado: Balanced worker’s lunch with meat or fish, rice, beans, plantains, and queso frito.
  • • Chifrijo: Sizzling pork cracklings over tender red beans, avocado slices, and chimichurri.
🕯️ Living Lore & Curiosity

The neighboring Nicoya Peninsula is one of Earth's rare Blue Zones, where community members routinely pass 100 years of age with high vitality, nourished by active movement and social purpose.

🥾 Essential Experiences

Canopy walk across suspension bridges; spotting the Resplendent Quetzal and sloths; bathing in volcanic thermal waters near Arenal.

7-Day Estimated Total $1,490 USD
Flight (Est.) $420 USD
Hotel / night $80 - $140
Food / day $30 - $50
🇵🇦 Panama • Two Oceans Central America

Casco Antiguo & Guna Yala

Interoceanic crossroads and indigenous Caribbean autonomy

Why It Is Essential

The vital land bridge connecting the Americas. Unites the metropolitan and colonial charm of Casco Antiguo with the sacred archipelago of San Blas (Guna Yala), 365 coral islands governed with total autonomy by the Guna people.

🍲 Ancestral Flavors
  • • Sancocho Panameño: Chicken soup simmered with native culantro herb and hearty ñame yams.
  • • Rondón: Caribbean seafood chowder simmered in fresh coconut milk, plantains, and sweet peppers.
  • • Carimañolas: Crispy mashed cassava croquettes stuffed with seasoned ground beef.
🕯️ Living Lore & Curiosity

Guna women craft Molas, intricate multi-layered reverse appliqué textiles portraying rainforest cosmology that helped preserve their sovereignty in the 1925 Dule Revolution.

🥾 Essential Experiences

Watching container ships rise through Miraflores Locks on the Panama Canal; island-hopping in San Blas; rooftop dining in Casco Viejo.

7-Day Estimated Total $1,420 USD
Flight (Est.) $430 USD
Hotel / cabana $75 - $130
Food / day $25 - $45
🇳🇮 Nicaragua • Great Lakes Central America

Granada & Ometepe Island

Twin volcanic cones rising from a freshwater sea

Why It Is Essential

An hourglass-shaped island formed by two volcanoes (Concepción and Maderas) in Lake Cocibolca. Next door, Granada is among Central America’s most colorful and welcoming colonial settlements.

🍲 Ancestral Flavors
  • • Vigorón: Steamed cassava topped with crunchy pork chicharrón and pickled cabbage on banana leaves.
  • • Nacatamal: Festive corn dough steamed with seasoned pork, potatoes, rice, and prunes in banana leaves.
  • • Frescos de Cacao con Leche: Creamy drink of roasted cacao beans with cinnamon and toasted grains.
🕯️ Living Lore & Curiosity

Lake Ometepe is the only freshwater lake in the world harboring adapted bull sharks. Across the island, hundreds of pre-Columbian petroglyphs remain carved into sacred basalt stones.

🥾 Essential Experiences

Kayaking the tranquil Rio Istián wetlands between volcanoes; swimming in Ojo de Agua springs; boating Granada’s 365 Isletas.

7-Day Estimated Total $1,190 USD
Flight (Est.) $450 USD
Hotel / night $40 - $80
Food / day $15 - $30

Belonging & Best Practices

Traveling as a Conscious Cultural Ally

For MongoDB, traveling means practicing empathy, patronizing local family businesses, and honoring indigenous communities who have stewarded these lands for millennia.

🌾

Buy Directly from Artisans

When acquiring alpaca textiles in Peru or a Mola in Panama, respect the matrilineal time and lineage of weaving. Negotiate gently and acknowledge that handmade artifacts represent days of dedicated craft.

🌱

Preserve Sacred Sanctuaries

Many archaeological complexes, volcanic craters, and lagoons are active altars for Quechua, Aymara, and Maya elders. Always ask permission before photographing individuals and leave rocks, fossils, and plants intact.

💬

Learn Indigenous Words

Sharing a sincere "Pura Vida" in Costa Rica, saying "Sulpayki" (thank you in Quechua), or greeting elders respectfully fosters human connections far more rewarding than any tour bus could provide.
